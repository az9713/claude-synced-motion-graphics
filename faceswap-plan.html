Face Swap Plan — stock_report

2026-08-19 · project claude_design_part_N_zinho · status: executed 2026-08-19 — result: videos/stock_report/output_professor.mp4

Goal

Replace the talking head in stock_report.MOV (10-second selfie clip, 1080×1920, 30 fps) with a fictional retired Ivy League professor: white male, early 70s, 1970s–80s MIT style. The process runs autonomously end to end. The reference face is AI-generated. Budget ceiling: $10 fal.ai credit. Expected spend: ≈ $1.55 (worst case with one retry ≈ $3.10).

Models researched (schemas verified against the live fal.ai API)

fal-ai/wan/v2.2-14b/animate/replace RECOMMENDED

Wan 2.2 Animate, replace mode. Replaces the whole character. Preserves the scene's lighting, motion, and facial expressions — lip sync carries over. Required inputs: video_url + image_url (character reference).

fal-ai/pixverse/swap FALLBACK

Mode person replaces the whole person. Keeps original audio (original_sound_switch default true). Max 720p. Price for the 10-second clip: $0.40 at 720p ($0.20 per 5 s, doubled over 5 s). Use if Wan quality disappoints.

fal-ai/face-swap/video-to-video SET ASIDE

Swaps the face only. Hair, clothes, and body stay from the source. Keeps native resolution. Price unpublished (older utility model, typically cents — first run measures it). Rejected: a professor face on the original clothing mismatches the persona.

easel-ai/advanced-face-swap REJECTED

Image input only — no video support. Out.

Accepted trade-off

All person-replacement models cap output at 720p. The swapped clip drops from 1080×1920 to 720p-class. The five overlay cards re-render on top at full sharpness — they are HTML layers in videos/stock_report/public/, not video pixels.

Reference face — fictional, consent-safe

1970s–80s MIT archive material (Getty ~2,171 MIT professor photos; MIT Museum; MIT Libraries) informs the text prompt only. No real professor's photo enters the swap. The face is generated with Nano Banana on fal (fal-ai/nano-banana, ≈ $0.04), waist-up — Wan wants a character reference, not a face crop.

"waist-up portrait of a retired professor, white male in his early 70s, gray receding hair, trimmed gray beard, horn-rimmed glasses, brown tweed jacket with elbow patches, knit wool tie, warm office light, bookshelves behind, 1980s Kodachrome film look"

Execution pipeline (autonomous on "go")

  1. Generate the professor image with fal-ai/nano-banana. Save to generations/refs/ with a prompt sidecar JSON.
  2. Upload the image and stock_report.MOV to fal storage. The model needs URLs, not local paths.
  3. Call fal-ai/wan/v2.2-14b/animate/replace at 720p. Poll until complete. Download the result immediately to generations/.
  4. Verify: file exists and has size; probe frames to see the professor; check the audio track.
  5. If audio was dropped, remux it from the original with ffmpeg (stream copy, no re-encode).
  6. Point the HyperFrames project at the swapped clip, then re-render:
    cd videos/stock_report && npx hyperframes render public --skill=talking-head-recut -o output.mp4 --fps 30
  7. Report: files created, measured spend vs the $10 ceiling.

Autonomy note: the genmedia per-video "quote and go" gate is set aside by the user's explicit instruction. The $10 credit is the hard ceiling; spend is tracked across every call.
